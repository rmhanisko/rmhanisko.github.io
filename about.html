I believe the best results come from building a true partnership with business owners. Every business operates differently, so understanding your goals and day-to-day challenges is an important part of creating solutions that actually work.

I leverage the experience, structure, and problem-solving approaches I learned in enterprise environments and apply them in practical ways that make sense for independent businesses.
