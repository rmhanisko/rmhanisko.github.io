RMH Solutions • Digital Promotions Platform

Privacy Policy

Effective Date: November 20, 2026

1. Overview

RMH Solutions ("we," "us," or "our") operates the Digital Promotions Platform, which allows customers to obtain and redeem promotional offers from participating businesses.

The Platform does not require customers to create accounts or provide their names, email addresses, phone numbers, or payment information to obtain a promotion. This Privacy Policy describes how information is handled when the Platform is used.

2. Information We Process

The Platform processes information needed to issue, validate, redeem, and report on promotions, including:

  • Unique promotion codes and identifiers.
  • Promotion issuance, expiration, and redemption dates.
  • Promotion status, such as available, redeemed, or expired.
  • Business and campaign identifiers.
  • Staff authentication information used for authorized redemption and reporting.

Hosting and infrastructure providers may also process technical information, such as IP addresses, browser information, and request logs, when delivering the Platform.

3. Browser Storage

The Platform uses browser local storage to remember promotions obtained on a particular browser and device. This helps display a previously issued promotion when the customer returns.

Local storage used for this purpose is not an advertising cookie. Clearing browser data or switching browsers or devices may prevent the Platform from retrieving a previously issued promotion.

4. How Information Is Used

Information is processed to generate and display promotions, validate promotion codes, prevent repeat redemption, maintain issuance and redemption records, provide participating businesses with campaign reporting, and support Platform security and reliability.

5. Service Providers

RMH Solutions uses third-party technology providers, including Supabase, for database, storage, and application functionality. These providers may process technical and operational information as necessary to deliver their services.

6. Information Sharing

Authorized personnel of participating businesses may access promotion records and campaign performance information relevant to their businesses. We may also disclose information when required by applicable law or when reasonably necessary to protect the Platform and its users.

Customers are not required to provide identifying contact information to obtain promotions.

7. Data Retention

Promotion records may be retained for operational reporting, redemption verification, troubleshooting, and security. Retention periods may vary based on operational needs and applicable legal requirements.

8. Data Security

We use technical and organizational measures intended to protect Platform information against unauthorized access, alteration, or disclosure. No internet-based system can guarantee absolute security.

9. Children's Privacy

The Platform is not specifically directed toward children under 13 and does not intentionally request personal information from children.

10. Changes to This Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy to reflect changes to Platform functionality, business practices, or applicable requirements. The effective date will be updated when revisions are published.

11. Contact

For privacy-related questions, contact RMH Solutions at info@rmhsolutions.io or visit rmhsolutions.io.
